Our Build Standard

What ships by default, not as an upsell.

  • Core Web Vitals budgets — LCP under 2.5s, INP under 200ms, CLS under 0.1 on a mid-range phone.
  • Semantic HTML — one H1 per page, logical heading order, real links and buttons.
  • Structured data — Organization, Service, FAQ and Breadcrumb markup that matches visible content.
  • Accessibility basics — labelled forms, keyboard navigation, focus states, contrast, alt text.
  • Indexing hygiene — clean URLs, self-referencing canonicals, XML sitemap, sensible robots rules.
  • Measurement — analytics and conversion events verified before launch, not after.

Redesigns Without Losing Rankings

The migration checklist that stops a new site from erasing an old one's progress.

URL inventory before design. Every indexed URL, its traffic and its inbound links, exported and classified.

One-to-one redirect map. Each old URL points at its closest new equivalent — not at the homepage. Chains and loops are tested before launch.

Content parity review. Titles, H1s and body copy are compared page by page, because a redesign that quietly deletes a ranking page is the most common own goal in SEO.

Staged crawl. The new build is crawled on a staging domain and checked for status codes, canonicals, missing assets and broken internal links.

Post-launch monitoring. Sitemap resubmitted, coverage watched for four weeks, and ranking movements compared against the pre-launch baseline.

How long does a website project take?
A focused marketing site typically runs four to eight weeks from approved structure to launch. Ecommerce and multi-language builds take longer. The biggest variable is usually content and approvals, not development.
Do you write the copy?
We can. Most projects need page briefs, headings and on-page copy written around search intent, and we would rather do that than build beautiful pages with placeholder text.
Can you work with our existing site instead of rebuilding it?
Often yes. If the platform is sound, a targeted performance and conversion pass is cheaper than a rebuild. If it is failing Core Web Vitals or can't support the structure you need, we will say so with evidence.
Who hosts the site, and what happens after launch?
You do — hosting, domain and analytics remain in your accounts. We handle deployment and can stay on a light monthly retainer for updates, monitoring and improvements.
